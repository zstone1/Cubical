The route is the garside one; generators the simples `1^n -> [n]`, relations garside's. Artin is a
restatement on top. Items off the route are at the end.

"Presented" always means `Presentation C` (`Machinery/Presentation/Basic`) — a 2-polygraph over `C`:
0-cells `V` with `ob : V → C`, 1-cells `Gen` with `arrow` into the arrows of `C`, 2-cells a relation
on words. The cells *index*; they are not the objects and arrows. That is what makes `transport`
free, so one presentation is read on every equivalent category rather than a second being built
there and compared. Obligations: `spans` (full), `complete` (faithful), `covers` (ess. surj.).
Ways to get one: `presentedMonoidPresentation` (from a `PresentedMonoid`), `Presentation.elements`
(lift along a discrete fibration), `transport` (along an equivalence), and `Presentation.localize`
(OPEN — `Cubical-mzl`). Everything is stated on the *opposite* — a word runs in refinement order
only there; `Presentation.op` is `Cubical-06q`.

The route below is the **Garside** one. The Artin presentation, and the 3-skeleton theorem it

Ch(Z) stuff

5. Define `artinAtom n m`, `m : Fin (n-1)`, the codim 1 map `1^n -> \/b` flipping the m coordinate — `atomOnes`, target `atomComp`; `Cut.exists_eq_atom`

Injectivity is the retraction `posGrade`, `f |-> Graded.Germ.hom posGerm (crossPerm f)`, on no double cross. The isomorphism *implies* the simples generate and respect the garside relations.

Lifting stuff
9. **`⟨a | r⟩` presents `Ch(Z)[W⁻¹]` ⟹ `⟨pullback a | pullback r⟩` presents `Ch(K)[W⁻¹]`, for every K.** No wedge ~ tensor anywhere, before or after localizing. Three unconditional inputs: `Ch K = ∫(wedgeHoms K)` over `Ch Z` (`chEquivElements`), so a presentation pulls back (`Presentation.elements`, `chPresentation`); the merges are exactly the codimension-one members of `W` (`merge_iff`), hence a subfamily of the cut generators, and `W_K` is the inverse image of `W_Z` (`W_eq_inverseImage_toChZ`), so upstairs the *pullback of that subfamily* generates `W_K` — a cartesian lift of a word of merges is a word of lifted merges; and localizing a presented category at some of its generators adjoins formal inverses and the two inverse laws and nothing else. OPEN, only because the third input is: it is `Presentation.localize`, whose proof sits unbuilt in the orphaned `Machinery/Localization/PresentedLocalization` (`Cubical-mzl`). The tree currently reaches the localized presentation the long way round, through 10.
10. `InvertsMerges` is not for 9. It is for the strictly stronger statement 12 wants: that `Ch K[W⁻¹]` is *again a discrete fibration* over `Ch Z[W⁻¹]`, with the same fibre `wedgeHoms K a` — "one copy per fibre", which is what turns the presentation into an `ActionCategory` on `n!` objects rather than on all chains. A discrete fibration over `Ch Z` is a presheaf on `Ch Z`, and a presheaf descends along `Ch Z ⟶ Ch Z[W⁻¹]` exactly when it inverts `W`; `InvertsMerges K` is that condition verbatim (`((W Zbp).op).IsInvertedBy (wedgeHoms K)`), and is what `isLocalization_elementsDescent` consumes. At a merge it unwinds to "K is local for `⋁ ⟶ ⊗`", i.e. Segal — `invertsMerges_of_isSegal`, `isSegal_iff_invertsMerges_repoint` (an iff), `isLocalization_chDescent`. It fails at `K = □²`: the two staircases `s₁, s₂` and the 2-cell `c` carry one lifted merge `s₁ ⟶ c` and one lifted atom `s₂ ⟶ c`, so inverting leaves an arrow `s₂ ⟶ s₁` *inside a single fibre* — the presentation lift is untouched by this, the discreteness is not (`not_invertsMerges_cube_two`).

Working in Ch(H(cube n))
11. H(cube n) has wedge ~ tensor — `isSegal_H_cube`. **This is what `H` is for.** The face comparison has two halves and they do different jobs: **injective** (`IsSeparated`, `IsSegalSep`) makes a lift *unique*, **surjective** makes it *exist*, and only the first is what a presentation transfers along. The trichotomy, all four witnesses in the tree:

    | | injective | surjective |
    |---|---|---|
    | `cube n` | ✓ | ✗ `not_isSegal_cube_two` |
    | `H Z ≅ runBp` | ✗ `not_injective_faceComparison_H_Z` | ✓ `surjective_faceComparison_H_Z` |
    | `K × H Z` | ✗ `not_separatesMerges_prod_runBp` | ✗ |
    | `H(cube n)` | ✓ | ✓ `isSegal_H_cube` |

    So "why not `cube n × H(Z)`, whose chains are the same (`chSymChStarEquiv`)?": the product is *worse than either factor*. It attaches the order to nothing — `(square, order₁)` and `(square, order₂)` have the same restriction, since the run factor on `⋁[1,1]` is a subsingleton — so it fails the half that makes lifts unique, for every `K`. `H(K)` attaches the order to the **cell**, so the two decorated squares restrict to the two different staircases; that is the twist, and `not_desym_natural` is the same fact as a non-splitting. On the chain level: `W` does not transport across `Ch(K × H Z) ≅ Ch(H K)`. At `□²` both are the free category on `K₂,₂`, but `W` is a perfect matching in one and a star in the other, and the localizations differ — `End` is infinite in `Ch(H □²)[W⁻¹]` and trivial in `Ch(□² × H Z)[W⁻¹]`. `SeparatesMerges` (`separatesMerges_of_isSegalSep`) is what `Machinery/Presentation/Partial` consumes.
12. The presentation lifts along the fibration, one copy per fibre — `chLocEquivElements` (generic in K), `hLocEquiv`. The fibre *is* S_n — `fibreEquiv`.
13. This gives the braid structure, along the presentation — `hLocEquiv : Ch(H cube n)[W^-1] ~ ActionCategory (PosBraid n) (Perm (Fin n))`, garside generators and relations, one copy per ordering. Artin on top via `actionCategoryCongr (posBraid_equiv_artinPos n)` — `hLocArtinEquiv`. The presentation and the action category are **one object, not two**: `hLocPresentation = (germPresentation n).elements (runFibreOf …)` is the pullback of the base presentation along the (now-descended) fibration, and `hLocActionPresentation = (hLocPresentation n).transport (hLocEquiv n).op` is that same 2-polygraph read on the action category. `Presentation.val_eval` names the arrow a generating word is.
