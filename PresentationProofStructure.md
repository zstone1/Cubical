restatement on top. Items off the route are at the end.

Generic W stuff

Ch(Z) stuff
4. Every `\/ a` has a unique map `1^(dimSum a) -> \/ a` in W; call it `runMerge` — `existsUnique_W_ones`, `eq_runMerge`
5. Define `artinAtom n m`, `m : Fin (n-1)`, the codim 1 map `1^n -> \/b` flipping the m coordinate — `atomOnes`, target `atomComp`; `Cut.exists_eq_atom`
6. `runBraid : PosBraid n -> End(1^n)` sends sigma to `conj` of the simple `1^n -> [n]` realising it; prove it is an isomorphism — `runBraidEquiv`. Injectivity is the retraction `posGrade`, `f |-> Graded.Germ.hom posGerm (crossPerm f)`, on no double cross. The isomorphism *implies* the simples generate and respect the garside relations.
7. OPEN. Geometric height: `length(sigma) = factorization_size(runBraid sigma)`, read off the localization rather than transported back along `runBraid^-1`.
8. A presentation for Ch(Z)[W^-1], generated by `U_n {[1^n] -> [n]}` with garside relations at each n — `strandComponentGarside`, `runBraidEquiv`. Artin on top via `posBraid_equiv_artinPos` — `strandComponentArtin`, `runArtinEquiv`. Natural indexing of the relations needs 7.

Lifting stuff
